## 2. Synthesized Research Gap (Verified Against Literature)

Across these 16 verified studies, a sharp **disciplinary split** persists:
1. **Systems/Control-Plane Protection papers** (*AVANT-GUARD*, *FloodGuard*, *FlowRanger*, *FTMaster*, *FloRa*) engineer switch/controller mechanics against `Packet-In` floods and table overflow, **without** profiling ML/DL feature extraction or inference inside the controller.
2. **AI-for-SDN IDS papers** (*InSDN*, *LUCID*, *Dadhania et al.*, *Liu et al.*, *Hassan et al.*) either stay strictly offline on `CICFlowMeter` CSVs (which suffer both post-hoc observability mismatch and documented artifact leakage per *Engelen et al.*/*Lanvin et al.*), benchmark standalone inference outside an OpenFlow event loop, or report coarse averages in Ryu without decomposing **where** live overhead comes from (`Packet-In` base handling vs. feature extraction vs. model inference vs. Python cooperative event-loop/GIL blocking vs. flow-table refusals) and without measuring **new-flow legitimate connection collapse**.
